Cloud Characteristics

Five traits define cloud computing. Nail resource pooling — the one you missed on the pre-assessment.

The win

By the end of this lesson you'll know the five essential cloud characteristics, and that "many clients and devices accessing pooled resources" is resource pooling.

The five essential characteristics

CharacteristicWhat it meansExam tell
On-demand self-serviceProvision resources yourself, no human contact with the provider"self-service, no provider interaction"
Broad network accessAvailable over the network from standard devices"phones, laptops, workstations"
Resource poolingProvider pools resources to serve many consumers (multi-tenant)"various clients/devices access pooled resources"
Rapid elasticityScale up or down quickly; resources seem unlimited"scale quickly / on demand"
Measured serviceUsage is metered, monitored, and billed"track usage and bill"

Resource pooling means the provider combines (pools) its computing resources and serves many consumers at once through a multi-tenant model. Different clients and devices draw from the same shared pool, with resources assigned and reassigned as demand shifts.

Measured service means the provider meters and monitors your usage — so organizations can track consumption and be billed accordingly.

The trap

Two phrases look similar. "Various clients and devices access resources" = resource pooling (many tenants share the pooled resources). "Track usage and bill" = measured service (metering/billing). Don't swap them.
