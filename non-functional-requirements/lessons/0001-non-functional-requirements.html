Non-functional Requirements

How well a system behaves, not just what it does. Four words describe the system's quality.

The win

By the end of this lesson you'll know availability means the system is accessible at a specified time (Q21), and how to tell it apart from reliability, usability, and scalability.

The four NFRs

NFRQuestion it answersExam tell
AvailabilityCan users reach it when they need it?"accessible at a specified time"
ReliabilityDoes it work correctly over time?"correct, consistent, without failure"
UsabilityIs it easy to use?"ease of use"
ScalabilityCan it grow with demand?"handle growth / more load"

Availability is about uptime and reachability — the likelihood the system is accessible to the user at a specified time, whether peak or off-peak. A critical mission project that must be reachable during peak and off-peak hours is an availability requirement.

The trap

Availability and reliability are easy to swap. Availability = "is it reachable right now?" Reliability = "does it work correctly without failing over time?" The word "accessible" points to availability.
