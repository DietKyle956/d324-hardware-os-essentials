# Mission: Pass the WGU D324 Objective Assessment (Hardware and Operating Systems Essentials)

## Why
The user is a WGU student preparing for the D324 objective assessment. They have already taken the pre-assessment (`d324_oa_topics_and_questions.md`) and scored "Competent" in all three competency areas, but missed 15 of 60 questions. The goal is to close those gaps and pass the real OA with confidence.

## Success looks like
- Pass the D324 OA with all three competencies at "Competent" or above
- Correctly answer every question type that was missed on the pre-assessment (see the priority queue in `ROADMAP.md`)
- Have a set of quick-reference cheat sheets in `./reference/` for last-minute review

## Constraints
- Self-paced, spread across multiple sessions in this workspace
- Study material is the WGU course (lrps.wgu.edu provision links) and this coaching report
- No formal computer-science background assumed; concepts are taught from first principles

## Out of scope
- Computer-science theory beyond what the OA tests
- Programming/software-engineering practice (the OA needs conceptual fluency, not code)
- CompTIA A+ exam prep as a separate goal (A+ overlaps but is not the target)
