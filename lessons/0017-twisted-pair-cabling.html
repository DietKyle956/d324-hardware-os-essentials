Twisted-Pair Cabling: Category Ratings

Cat ratings map to speeds. Memorize the early ones — especially Cat 3 at 10 Mbps.

The win By the end of this lesson you'll know Category 3 = 10 Mbps (Q34), and the full ladder of Cat ratings up to 10 Gbps.

The Category ladder

CategorySpeedTypical use
Cat 1Voice onlyAnalog telephone
Cat 24 MbpsLegacy token ring
Cat 310 Mbps10BASE-T Ethernet
Cat 416 MbpsToken ring
Cat 5100 MbpsFast Ethernet
Cat 5e1 Gbps (1000 Mbps)Gigabit Ethernet
Cat 61 Gbps (10 Gbps to 55 m)Gigabit / 10G short runs
Cat 6a10 Gbps10 Gigabit Ethernet

Category 3 cable was the standard for early Ethernet and carries data at up to 10 Mbps. That's the anchor to remember: Cat 3 → 10 Mbps.

The trap The 10 Mbps mark sits at Cat 3, not Cat 1 or Cat 2 (both below it) and not Cat 4 (16 Mbps). Anchor the pair "Cat 3 = 10 Mbps" and the surrounding ratings fall into place: Cat 5 = 100, Cat 5e = 1000.
