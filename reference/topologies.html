Cheat Sheet — Topologies

TopologyShapeKey fact
BusOne backbone cableCheap & easy, but hard to reconfigure
StarCentral hub/switchEasy to manage; center is single point of failure
RingLoop, two neighborsOrderly data flow
MeshMany interconnectionsMultiple pathways, fault-tolerant, expensive

Memory hook

Bus = cheap but rigid. Mesh = many paths. Star = central box. Ring = loop.
