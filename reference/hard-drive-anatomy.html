Cheat Sheet — Hard Drive Anatomy

PartDefinitionCommon mix-up
PlattersRigid circular disks coated in magnetic materialThe physical medium — this is where data lives
Read/write headsElectromagnetic devices on an actuator arm; float just above the surfaceThey read/write — they do not store
TracksConcentric circles on a platterOrganization, not a medium
SectorsPie-shaped slices of a trackSmallest addressable unit (512 B or 4 KB)
SpindleMotor that spins the platters—
Actuator armPositions the heads across the platter—

Memory hook

Platters are the medium; tracks and sectors are the map; heads do the work.
