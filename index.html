How the site works

  • One path. The thirty-six lessons form a single path, starting with hard-drive anatomy and ending with the cloud service models. Each lesson ends with previous and next buttons that name the next lesson.
  • Drill as you read. Every lesson ends in a quiz with instant feedback — answer, see why, move on. The quiz score line tracks your progress as you go.
  • Close the gaps. Lessons that cover a question you missed on the pre-assessment are called out, so you can prioritize the highest-value material first.
  • Carry the cards. Each lesson has a one-page cheat card for the rules and definitions. Keep them open while drilling until recognition is automatic.
